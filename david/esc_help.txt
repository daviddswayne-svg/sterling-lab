ESC FAMILY HISTORY EXPLORER - HOW TO USE IT
What AI David knows about using the Family History Explorer at swaynesystems.ai/esc/. This is only how the app
works; AI David cannot see or search the family database. This file is public (the repo is public).

WHAT IT IS
- A private site for Swayne family members to explore the family history database in plain English: about 144,000
  photos, 3,000 people and 6,900 trips over 140+ years. You type a question, and the app writes the database query
  for you and shows the answer.
- It runs on David's own Mac Studio with the Gemma 4 model. If nobody has used it for a while, the first question
  can take about a minute while the model loads; after that answers are quick.

SIGNING IN
- Two tabs: Login and Register.
- Login: your username and password. Tick "Keep me signed in" to stay signed in on that device.
- Register: you need your RDX ID (your number in the family database), your first name or nickname exactly as it
  appears in the database, a username you choose, and a password of at least 6 characters (typed twice). If you
  don't know your RDX ID, ask the real David; AI David never gives out RDX IDs.
- Common register messages: "Please fill in all fields", "Passwords do not match", "Password must be at least 6
  characters". If registration still fails, check the name matches the database and the RDX ID is right, or ask
  David.

THE SIDEBAR (after you sign in)
- Your name, and a Logout button.
- Change Password: current password, then the new one twice.
- My Activity: Load Activity shows your login history and past questions; Clear Query History deletes them.
- Mode: pick Photos, Journals, Trip Map or Family Tree. Switching modes clears the chat.
- Connection: shows whether the family database and the model are ready ("Thinking..." while it works).
- Database Schema: a picture of how the tables connect, with a full-size button.
- Stop: cancels a question that is still running. Clear Chat: starts over.
- Dashboard: back to the Swayne Systems home page.

PHOTOS MODE (the main explorer)
- Ask anything about the family history in plain English: people, photos, trips, places, species, dates.
- Results come back as a TABLE by default, with all the rows. Say "summarize" or "give me an overview" for a
  written summary instead.
- Photos appear only when you ask for them: add "show photos" or "show images". Each photo has a full-size button
  and a map-pin button (shows where it was taken, when a location is recorded; locations are approximate). Big
  sets show 25 at a time with a "Load 25 more" button.
- "Climbing trips" means officially tagged climbing expeditions (best for elevation questions); "trips" includes
  everything.
- Decades work naturally: "trips in the 1970s", "photos from the 1990s".
- Names and nicknames both work ("Mike" or "Michael"). A full name goes straight to the answer. A first name on its
  own often matches several people, so the app lists the likeliest matches and asks which one you meant; reply
  with the name you want.
- Each answer has an expandable "SQL Queries" section showing exactly how the database was searched.

JOURNALS MODE (Mike's Journal Magazine)
- Mike Swayne's trip journals from the 1930s to the 2020s: mountaineering, fishing, road trips, wildlife surveys
  and family travel.
- Browse by topic or place, for a list with excerpts: "Find fishing trip journals from the 1960s".
- Open one journal as a photo magazine, with the day-by-day story and photos: "Tell me about the [trip name]".
- Search the journal text by keyword: "Find journals that mention bears".
- If you say "Michael" or "Elizabeth" it asks which one, because those names are shared across generations.

TRIP MAP MODE
- Name a specific trip ("Show me the map for the [year] [trip name]"), or use a trip number: "Map trip 640".
- If several trips match, it lists them so you can pick one, then say "Map trip [number]".
- The map shows the GPS route line when one exists (otherwise a dashed line between waypoints), waypoints with name
  and elevation, and camera icons you can click to see photos taken there.

FAMILY TREE MODE
- An ancestry (pedigree) chart that starts from you. There is no chat in this mode. Use "Open Full Screen" for a
  bigger view.

GOOD HABITS
- Be specific: a full name, a year or decade, a place.
- If an answer looks wrong, rephrase with more detail, or open "SQL Queries" to see what was searched.
- If a question is taking too long, press Stop and try a narrower question.
